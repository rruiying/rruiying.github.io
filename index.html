About me

I am a first-year M.Sc. student in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence (RCI) at the Technical University of Munich. I am interested in physical AI and spatial AI — how embodied agents build a geometric, persistent understanding of the world and act in it — and in robot learning as the bridge between the two.

I am also a computer graphics enthusiast. Rendering, geometry processing and differentiable simulation are, to me, the same mathematics that underpins spatial perception seen from the other direction, and I enjoy working where the two meet.

Before the master's, I worked as a student assistant (HiWi) at the Associate Professorship of Embedded Systems and Internet of Things (TUM-ESI), building AR / Web-of-Things digital-twin demos. My bachelor's thesis there — TUM-ESI-Universal-XR, a cross-platform Unity XR package unifying fragmented OpenXR vendor extensions — was graded 1.0.

I am currently looking for research and internship opportunities in these areas. If you think there is a fit, please get in touch.

Research interests: physical AI · spatial AI · robot learning · 3D vision and scene representation · computer graphics and rendering
