I am an M.Sc. student in Electrical and Information Engineering at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). My work lies at the intersection of embedded systems, robotics, mixed reality, and intelligent cyber-physical systems. My research centers on learning-based world models, deep reinforcement learning, and vision-based embodied intelligence. I am particularly interested in integrating geometry, optimization, and dynamical systems theory with machine learning for 2D/3D perception, graphics and rendering, signal processing, and control. My bachelor’s thesis investigated interoperability frameworks for Web of Things (WoT)-based digital twins in augmented reality environments. I am currently working as a student assistant (HiWi) at the Associate Professorship of Embedded Systems and Internet of Things at TUM. Alongside my research, I continuously strengthen my mathematical foundations in machine learning, convex optimization, signal and systems theory, and control, aiming to develop a rigorous and unified framework for intelligent embodied systems. I am open to collaboration and academic exchange in these areas.

Research Interests: Signal processing, Control Theory, embedded systems, mixed reality, robotics, learning-based world models, deep reinforcement learning, vision-based embodied intelligence
