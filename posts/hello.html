Hello, and what this blog is for

23 August 2026 · Meta

I keep notes anyway, so they may as well be readable. This is where course write-ups, paper summaries and small experiments end up — mostly around physical AI, spatial AI, robot learning and graphics.

The archive is a flat list: every entry has a date, a category and a few tags, and the index lets you search across all of them or sort by date, title or category. There are no comments and no way to post from the site itself; entries are added by committing to the repository.

Most of what follows will be Course notes from the RCI master's at TUM. They are written for my future self, so expect gaps and the occasional wrong turn.
