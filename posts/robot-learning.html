Robot Learning — lecture notes

20 August 2026 · Course notes

Running notes from the TUM Robot Learning lecture. Updated as the semester goes on.

1. Policy representations

A policy maps observations to actions. The interesting design question is what structure to bake in: end-to-end networks, movement primitives, or an explicit model plus a planner on top.

2. Imitation learning

Behaviour cloning is supervised learning on demonstrations, and its failure mode is compounding distribution shift — small errors move the robot to states that were never demonstrated. DAgger addresses this by querying the expert on states the learner actually visits.

3. Model-based control

Learn the dynamics, then optimise actions against the learned model. Sample efficient, but the optimiser will happily exploit whatever the model gets wrong.
